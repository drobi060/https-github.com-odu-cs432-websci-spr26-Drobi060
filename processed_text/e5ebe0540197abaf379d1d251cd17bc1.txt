Introducing Interstride
Interstride is a digital platform designed to support international students in navigating your career development and job search in the U.S., while also offering resources for American students seeking global career opportunities. It combines career tools, networking capabilities, and immigration guidance in one user-friendly interface. Here are some of its key features:
What Interstride Offers:
Job and Internship Listings tailored to international students
Visa and Immigration Resources to help you navigate work authorization
Networking Opportunities with employers and global professionals
Career Development Tools to enhance your job search strategies
Sign in using your ODU email and password at Interstride .
