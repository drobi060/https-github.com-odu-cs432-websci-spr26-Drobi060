Outdoor Equipment Rental Center
The Outdoor Adventure Program offers a wide variety of equipment to help you enjoy the outdoors with all your Monarch friends. Visit the Outdoor Adventure Center to rent your equipment today! Rental equipment is available for ODU students, Student Recreation Center members, and non-member ODU faculty and staff.
Location
The Outdoor Equipment Rental Center is located in the Outdoor Adventure Center (OAC), within the ODU Student Recreation Center at 4700 Powhatan Avenue. Renters are asked to visit the OAC during open hours .
Questions?
For questions about outdoor equipment rentals, contact Jared Causby, Assistant Director of the Outdoor Adventure Program, at jcausby@odu.edu .
