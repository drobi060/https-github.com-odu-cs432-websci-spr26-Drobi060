J.C. Scrap Chandler Pool
Dive into the right aquatic program for you!
The J.C. Scrap Chandler Pool, located inside the ODU Student Recreation and Well-Being Center, is an 8 lane, 25 yard indoor pool with depths between 4 and 15 feet. The temperature is set between 78-80 degrees because it is a competition pool. Locker and shower facilities are available.
Members can participate in a variety of programs including:
Lap swimming
American Red Cross safety courses
Swimming lessons
Questions?
If you have questions about the pool or any of our programs, please email us at aquatics@odu.edu .
