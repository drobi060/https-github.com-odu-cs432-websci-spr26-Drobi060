Transfer Resources and Partnerships
We assist transfer students and students coming from High School with dual enrollment credit from community colleges with their transition to the university, providing them with resources for their success by collaborating with offices across campus. Our mission is to create a transfer inclusive culture that supports the successful transition, engagement, and academic achievement of transfer students. Our vision is to empower transfer students to be active, engaged, and responsible learners who fulfill their individual goals.
