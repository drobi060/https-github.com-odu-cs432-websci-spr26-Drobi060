Academic Advising
The Academic Advising team at Old Dominion University is committed to empowering students through personalized guidance, academic support, and strategic planning. We strive to foster student success by providing accessible resources, fostering a culture of lifelong learning, and equipping students with the tools to achieve their academic and career goals.
All degree-seeking undergraduate students have an assigned academic advisor and we encourage all students to meet with their academic advisor at least once per semester to ensure they are on track with their academic plan. Your advisor is excited to meet with you to ensure your course selections align with your academic plans, discuss academic and career goals, let you know about opportunities around campus, as well as connect you with resources for your academic success.​
You can find the name and contact information of your assigned academic advisor on your myODU portal , or schedule an appointment using the Monarch Advising Portal . ​
For questions, contact your advisor directly or email advisor@odu.edu !
