Michael and Kimthanh Lê Planetarium
Guiding the curiosity of Hampton Roads and beyond, the Michael and Kimthanh Lê Planetarium serves as a beacon of exploration, education, and inspiration. With a commitment to fostering a deeper understanding of the cosmos, we strive to ignite wonder and a lifelong passion for astronomy through captivating, immersive experiences. Our mission is to connect people of all ages to the mysteries of the cosmos and empower them to explore, discover, and appreciate the beauty of the Universe in which we live.

Weekly Public Planetarium Shows
Love space and enjoy stargazing? Ever wondered what it would be like to fall into a black hole? Come explore these mysteries at our weekly planetarium shows, perfect for families and recommended for ages 4+.
Shows typically last an hour and start with a full-dome movie. Each movie lasts about 20-25 minutes. Afterwards, we'll show the night sky including planets & constellations, fly through our solar system and visit other planets, traverse the Milkyway, and tour the Universe.
$5 per ticket. No affiliation with ODU is required to join the fun.
Bad Movie Night: hosted on the first Friday of each month, features classic sci-fi films from the 1950s and 60s that are so bad, they're good. The evening kicks off with a trivia contest, followed by the film screening. $5 per ticket.
