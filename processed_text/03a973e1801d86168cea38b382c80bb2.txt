Personal Training
Receive an individualized fitness program specifically designed for you. Our personal trainers deliver an efficient workout program with innovative ways to improve your health and fitness through instruction on correct form and technique. Each personal training package allows you and your trainer to set goals and a training plan to achieve them. Come train with us in person at the Student Recreation and Well-Being Center!

People Overcoming Obstacles with Exercise and Recreation (P.O.W.E.R.)
Participate in accessible and adapted recreational opportunities under the supervision of ODU doctoral physical therapy students. People Overcoming Obstacles with Exercise and Recreation (P.O.W.E.R.) is a program designed to serve the needs of those with special health conditions (i.e. heart disease, osteoporosis, arthritis, diabetes, physical and sensory limitations, post-rehabilitation, or other individual health concerns).
In order to participate, participants must obtain medical clearance from a doctor. After clearance is obtained, individualized programs are designed based on an initial assessment of the participant. From there, exercise assistance, supervision and progression is provided.
To learn more about P.O.W.E.R. please email us at recwell@odu.edu .

Women on Weights
FASST, or Faculty And Staff Strength Training, is a fitness program for ODU faculty and staff! This fun and supportive class focuses on building strength, enhancing overall health, and fostering a positive work/life balance. With a comprehensive and structured workout plan, you'll feel stronger and more refreshed for the day ahead. Plus, it's a great way to connect with colleagues and boost your fitness journey together. We need a minimum of 10 participants to get started, so grab a friend and sign up today!
FASST Meets Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays from 7-8 a.m. in the Student Recreation and Well-Being Center Studio A.
Spring Session 1:  February 2-March 6 (Registration Open January 20-February 2)
Spring Session 2: March 23-April 24 (Registration Open March 7-23)
Price: $45 for the 5-week session (That’s $3 per workout!)
Register Here
Women on Weights is a small group training program designed to ensure that female-identifying participants feel comfortable and confident in the weight room. The participants of WOW will take on the Student Recreation & Well-Being Center fit floor as a team alongside their coach. Throughout the session, team members focus on functional movement patterns designed to increase muscular strength and endurance for daily life activities, improved mobility, better posture, and increased neural control. If you are looking to become stronger, exercise more efficiently, and have fun in an inclusive atmosphere, this is the group for you.
Women On Weights is taking a rest. Check back soon for future offerings!
