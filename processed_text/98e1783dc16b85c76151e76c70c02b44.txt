Over 40 Years of Distance Learning Leadership
Old Dominion University is a public doctoral research institution and a national leader in distance learning with its main campus in Norfolk, VA. ODU has been a pioneer in technology-delivered learning since the mid-1980's.

Looking for a BSN but still need a RN license? You may be able to accelerate your path to a BSN with ODU's concurrent enrollment nursing program . In as little as two years, you could complete both an associate of applied science at a partner school and your BSN online from ODU.
