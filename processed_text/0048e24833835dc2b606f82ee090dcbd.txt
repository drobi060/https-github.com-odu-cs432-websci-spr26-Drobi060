Biography
Mohammad GhasemiGol is currently an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science and the School of Cybersecurity at Old Dominion University (ODU). He holds a Ph.D. in Computer Engineering in 2016 with a focus on cybersecurity. With over a decade of experience at the intersection of cybersecurity and artificial intelligence, his research spans intrusion detection and response systems, alert management systems, attack graphs, AI/GenAI security, and secure supply chains for AI systems. Before joining ODU, he was a research assistant professor at University of North Dakota and associate researcher at Imperial College London. He was also an assistant professor of computer engineering at the University of Birjand, where he held leadership roles as the IT Director and Director of the university’s Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT) for over five years.
Since joining ODU in August 2023, he has secured nearly $850,000 in competitive research and educational funding from agencies such as NSA and CCI, supporting projects on AI-powered cyber defense, supply chain security, deepfake detection, and AI security competitions. His current focus is on developing advanced AI techniques for real-world cybersecurity systems and enhancing the security of AI and GenAI systems, while teaching and mentoring students and contributing to national workforce development initiatives.
