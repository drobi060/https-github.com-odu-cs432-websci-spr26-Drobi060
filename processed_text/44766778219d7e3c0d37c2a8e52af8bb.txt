Home or Deployed, Your Degree is on Track
Military and veteran students and their dependents are welcome to apply for any degree program offered through ODUGlobal .
The online degree and certificate programs below are designed or recommended for military service members, with the possibility to gain academic credit for military training and experience.

Military Pathways to Shorten Your Time to a Degree
Your military training and experience may translate to academic credit for specific degrees at ODU. We have unique, signed military pathway agreements for 40+ degrees and certificates.
These pathways include options to earn credits from rank or training as well as CLEP and DANTES/DSST exams.
