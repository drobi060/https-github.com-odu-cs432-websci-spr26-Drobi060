Virginia Community College System (VCCS) with a Passport or Uniform Certificate of General Studies (UCGS)
Students who complete the Passport or Uniform Certificate of General Studies (UCGS) will receive equivalent credit for individual courses as shown in the ODU course equivalency database (a grade of C or higher is required to receive transfer credit). Credits will count towards General Education requirements at ODU, and students will be eligible for the Lower-Division General Education (LDGE) waiver once the General Education transfer requirements are fulfilled. Students must consult with their ODU advisor or the office of Transfer Resources & Partnerships upon enrollment to determine eligibility.
