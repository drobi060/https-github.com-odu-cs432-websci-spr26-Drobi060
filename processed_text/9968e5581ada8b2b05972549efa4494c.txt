Nutrition Education & Events
Nutrition 101 Workshops
In our Nutrition 101 Workshop, you will learn tips for consistently fueling your body with just the right foods, receive helpful tools to make it easier, and get support from fellow students from ODU's Registered Dietitian. Even in a group setting you'll get individual attention and quality answers to your nutrition questions. Register ►
Nutrition Corner
At Nutrition Corner, you can find our campus dietitian in various campus locations throughout the semester where she provides nutritional information and recipes! This is a drop-in event. No registration needed! Browse upcoming nutrition events on Monarch Groups.
Monarch Fuel
Get the most out of your workout by learning how to properly fuel your body! Studies show improvement to muscular strength in as little as 3 weeks when a few key eating strategies are put into place. Stop by the Monarch Fuel table located in various locations on campus to chat with ODU's campus dietitian about sports nutrition. This is a drop-in event. No registration needed! Browse upcoming nutrition events on Monarch Groups.
Monarch Fruit & Veggie Fest
It's all about the fruits and veggies! Learn which fruits and veggies are powerhouses that help build strong bodies. The Monarch Fruit & Veggie Fest includes a free farmer's market, chef demonstration using local produce, flowerpot crafts, herb planting, a fruit and veggie costume photo booth, music, games, and giveaways!
