October 2, 2023

The Office of Counseling Services, Student Health Services, and Health Promotion Office relocated from their locations in the Webb Student Center to the new construction next to the Student Recreation Center at 4700 Powhatan Avenue. With the addition of these new offices, the Student Recreation Center (SRC) is now called the Student Recreation & Well-Being Center (SRWC)!
The new Office of Counseling Services is located on the second floor of the Student Recreation & Well-Being Center in Suite 2403. Students can enter from the West Entry Walk, East Entry Walk, or Student Recreation Center (see image below) and take the stairs or elevator to the second floor. Questions? You may call the Office of Counseling Services at (757) 683-4401.
The new office for Student Health Services is located on the first floor of the Student Recreation & Well-Being Center in Suite 1402. Students can enter from the West Entry Walk, East Entry Walk, or Student Recreation Center (see image below). Signage has been placed in the hallways to assist with directions. Office hours remain the same in the new office, 8:00 a.m. - 5:00 p.m. You may call for an appointment at (757) 683-3132. After hours, you may reach on-call staff by calling ODU Police Dispatch at (757) 683-4000.
The new Health Promotion Office is located on the second floor of the Student Recreation & Well-Being Center in Suite 2026. Students can enter from the West Entry Walk, East Entry Walk, or Student Recreation Center (see image below) and take the stairs or elevator to the second floor. Questions? You may call the Health Promotion Office at (757) 683-5927.
Signage has been placed in the hallways to assist with directions.
