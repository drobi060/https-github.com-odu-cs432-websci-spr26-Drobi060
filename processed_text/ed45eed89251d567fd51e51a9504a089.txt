Transportation & Parking Services
The mission of Transportation and Parking Services is to provide a comprehensive program of high-quality transportation and parking services in a dynamic University setting. The department plans, implements, and assesses the University's transportation and parking program using technical innovations; professional and courteous customer service: safe procedures; and clear and effective communication.
Student Spring Parking Options
TPS will offer student parking permits on a semesterly, 30-day, weekly, or daily basis for the Spring 2026 Semester! Spring Semester permits will be available for purchase December 1 and will be valid 1/1/26 - 5/17/26.

You can use the ODU Parking Portal for most transactions. Purchase your parking permit, update vehicle information, appeal a citation and more!
