The fair will feature students from a wide range of business majors, including Accounting, Finance, Risk Management & Insurance, Marketing, Business Analytics, Information Systems, Supply Chain Management, and Management, among others. Employers will have the opportunity to recruit for full-time, part-time, internship, and co-op positions, making this an excellent opportunity to build your talent pipeline and strengthen your presence on campus.
Students of all majors are welcome to attend. However, many participating employers will be focused on candidates with business-related majors and skill sets. For students outside the business disciplines, the fair can still be a useful opportunity to practice networking, learn more about employers, and gain insight into professional expectations.
