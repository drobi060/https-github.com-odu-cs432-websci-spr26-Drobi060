Doctoral Candidate Sandra Abdellah Uses Research to Build Connections
Sandra Abdellah’s journey at Old Dominion University began with a spark ignited during her undergraduate years at Virginia Wesleyan University (VWU). That spark is now a passion for knowledge as a second-year Ph.D. student in the Department of Chemistry and Biochemistry at ODU, where she researches novel cancer therapies and global public health solutions.

Isaura Simões Research on Tick-Borne Diseases
Dr. Simões joined ODU in January 2024 and hit the ground running. In her lab, she collaborates with scientists globally and mentors ODU graduate students. One of her major goals is to bring awareness that Rickettsia remains comparatively understudied among intracellular pathogens and to advance strategies to prevent and treat infections transmitted by infected ticks.
