Fitness Certifications
Join the ODU Fit Fam! Becoming a group fitness instructor or personal trainer at ODU is free and flexible. Sign up for the online Canvas course to learn the fundamentals of being a group fitness instructor or personal trainer, take our in-house exam, and then participate in practical training to help you feel confident leading your first class or client. Once your training is complete, you'll see your name on our group fitness schedule or personal training webpage and can start changing lives!
If you're looking for a job that inspires others and helps them reach their goals, consider becoming a personal trainer or group exercise instructor at ODU!
