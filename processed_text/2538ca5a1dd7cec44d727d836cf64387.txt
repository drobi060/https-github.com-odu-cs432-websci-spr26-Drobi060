The ODU Graduate Catalog states that "Graduate students who complete their graduate degree or certificate within eight years following admission to Old Dominion University will follow the requirements in the catalog in effect at the time of their first enrollment."

Students should contact the Graduate Program Director if they are interested in discussing an update of their Catalog Year.
