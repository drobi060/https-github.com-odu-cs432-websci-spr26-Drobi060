Our Mission & Goals
The Office of International Collaborations (OIC) strengthens ODU's global presence by cultivating international partnerships and collaborations that advance the University’s academic and research initiatives. OIC seeks to drive a thorough internationalization program at ODU, fostering a diverse, inclusive, and sustainable global campus environment.
Mission & Goals:
Expand ODU's global presence and international partnerships.
Promote international experiences for faculty, staff, and students.
Develop international research and experiential learning opportunities.
Increase international content in course offerings.
Partner with other international offices on campus to support international students, faculty, and staff.
Partner with community organizations to enhance international exchanges that can benefit Norfolk and the Hampton Roads region.
