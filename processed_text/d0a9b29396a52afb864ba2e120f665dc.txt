Understand how leadership works in times of change and how anyone can lead without a formal title.
When you lead, you set an example. And when you set an example, you positively influence the lives of others. The Center for Career & Leadership Development has many leadership opportunities available. The Leadership Lecture Series offers leadership-focused programs throughout each semester to give students the opportunity to build on current leadership skills.
Start your leadership journey, sign up for the Leadership Lecture Series. Students who complete three or more sessions in a semester receive a Leadership Certificate and Medal for graduation.
