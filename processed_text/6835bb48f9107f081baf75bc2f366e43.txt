International Opportunities for Students
Highly engaged, passionate ODU students make excellent candidates for a wide variety of national-level scholarships. Some of the opportunities listed here require a university endorsement - a process that is managed through the Office of International Collaborations.
The list of opportunities below is not comprehensive and we welcome suggestions to continue to grow this list. If you are interested in any of the opportunities below, or in any other international scholarship that is not listed, please contact the Office of International Collaborations.
