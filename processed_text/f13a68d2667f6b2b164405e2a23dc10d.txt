Payment Details
Annual | Semester | Monthly
Annual memberships run for a full year (12 months) from the date of purchase and must be paid in full upfront at the time of purchase.
Semester memberships must be paid in full upfront at the time of purchase and run:
Fall: August 16 to December 31
Spring: January 1 - May 15
Summer: May 16 - August 15
Monthly memberships must be auto drafted from a valid credit or debit card, and will run continuously until the member takes action to terminate .
SRWC Parking Permit
$10/month special parking permit for Alumni & Affiliate SRWC members only
To purchase an SRWC Parking Permit, fill out the interest form and someone from the ODU Transportation & Parking (TPS) office will email you instructions.
SRWC Parking Permit holders can park in Lots 42, 43 & 49 (along Powhatan Avenue) between 6 a.m. - Midnight ( campus parking map ).
ODU students and faculty/staff are NOT eligible to purchase this permit.
You must have a valid SRWC membership to use this permit. If your SRWC membership terminates or expires, your SRWC parking permit will also expire at that time.
