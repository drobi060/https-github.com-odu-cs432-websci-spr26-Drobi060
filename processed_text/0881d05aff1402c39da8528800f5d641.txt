Your Choice
Students applying for on-campus admissions can use the ODU Application or the Common Application. The Common Application allows you to complete one application and designate it for multiple institutions.
Students interested in pursuing their degree online through ODUGlobal should visit the ODUGlobal Admissions site .
Our Commitment
Whichever method you choose, the admissions staff at Old Dominion University is committed to processing your application in a timely manner.
