Transfer Experience
Discover Your Pride: Monarch Orientation is a required, one-day orientation experience for new students. During Discover Your Pride , students will build connections with your community, assemble and interact with your distinctive success team, including Transfer Resources and Partnerships , and prepare for pathways to enrollment and engagement.
Check your ODU email for information about signing up for Discover Your Pride. The cost for guest participation is $50 per person and is due at the time of registration.
