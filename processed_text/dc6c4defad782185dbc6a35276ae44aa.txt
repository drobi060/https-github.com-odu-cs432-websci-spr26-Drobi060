Impact Learning Communities
Learning Communities are groups of students who connect what they are learning with what they want to do: their majors, career choices, and/or their hopes for a better world. Learning Community students take one or more courses together and are open to students living on- or off- campus.
Service-learning is a type of experiential learning which integrates community-based experiences and projects into a course's curriculum, and allows students to apply their classroom knowledge while addressing real world community issues.
Career Treks
Consider bringing your students to an employer to gain a better understanding of various professions and job culture. An ideal visit includes a tour of the work site/facility, a briefing about the company, and a discussion panel of employees. Many employers are happy to give back and promote their brand to prospective employees. The Center for Career and Leadership Development can assist with connecting with an employer host, establishing a schedule for the Trek, and transportation arrangements and costs. Contact the Experiential Learning Team at experientiallearning@odu.edu to enhance your classroom experience!
