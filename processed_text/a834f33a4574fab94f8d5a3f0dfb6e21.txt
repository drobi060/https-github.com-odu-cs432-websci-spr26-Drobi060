Rock Climbing Wall
The indoor rock climbing wall is located inside the Student Recreation Center, near the front desk.
Features:
25-foot high climbing wall designed to resemble natural rock features
Climbers can choose routes set by staff or climb the natural features of the wall.
Staff can guide climbers of all skill levels to navigate the 1,125 square feet space
Questions?
For questions about outdoor equipment rentals, contact Jared Causby, Assistant Director of the Outdoor Adventure Program, at jcausby@odu.edu .
Climbing Wall Orientation
During your first visit to the climbing wall, you will need to take an orientation clinic. This orientation will provide an overview of the climbing wall rules and policies. After completing your orientation, you are welcome to climb while being belayed by one of the climbing wall staff members.
Climbing Competitions
Join us for the Climb On rock climbing competition in the fall and bouldering competition in the spring! Come out for some friendly competition, food, music, prizes, and more! No experience is necessary. Sign Up ►
Third Thursday Climbing Socials
Participate in a monthly themed climbing night. We'll have music, food, and special decorations at the rock wall! No experience is necessary. Bring a friend and join us for a fun time! This is a drop-in event. No registration required.
