Class Descriptions
Les Mills BODYBALANCE™
Ideal for anyone and everyone, BODYBALANCE™ is a new generation yoga class that will improve your mind, your body and your life. You can expect to bend and stretch through a series of simple yoga moves, elements of Tai Chi and Pilates while an inspiring soundtrack plays in the background. Breathing control is a part of all the exercises, and instructors will always provide options for those just getting started.
Les Mills BODYCOMBAT™
Les Mills BODYCOMBAT™ is a high-energy, martial arts-inspired workout that combines punches and kicks with a science-backed cardio peak training method to deliver a full-body, non-contact fitness class. It incorporates moves from disciplines like Karate, Taekwondo, Boxing, and Kung Fu to build strength, coordination, and cardiovascular fitness while helping participants release stress.
Les Mills BODYPUMP™
The original barbell workout for anyone looking to get lean, toned, and fit...fast! Using light to moderate weights with high repetitions, BODYPUMP™ gives you a total body workout that burns calories, builds strength, and improves muscle endurance, all set to powerful music that keeps you motivated.
Cardio Kickboxing
Cardio
Strike, punch, and kick your way to greater cardio fitness! This class is packed with real kickboxing moves, bodyweight exercises, and some of the best music. You'll leave every class sweaty and ready to conquer anything!
Cardio Step
Energize your workout with this high-energy cardio step class! Using aerobic steps, you’ll move through fun, rhythmic combinations designed to get your heart pumping, improve coordination, and build endurance. Whether you’re a beginner or experienced with step, this class will challenge your cardio fitness while keeping things engaging and upbeat.
Core & More
Strength & Conditioning
This 30-minute workout will torch your core...and more! Our instructor will guide you through a strength and conditioning class using a variety of methods and tools to challenge all fitness levels.
Cycle
Cardio
Cycling is a fun, high energy cardio workout! This class can combine any variety of endurance, strength, intervals, and recovery. Your instructor will use uplifting music and imagery to guide you through class. You will always decide if and when to challenge yourself throughout the ride. Open to all fitness levels!
Gentle Yoga
Mind & Body
Slow and steady yoga is perfect for those interested in establishing perfect balance between strength and flexibility. You will use your breath and awareness to create harmony in your body and tap into your true potential of what you are capable of. Great for beginners and even the most advanced yogi. All levels are welcome!
HIIT Strength
Push your limits with this high-intensity interval training class focused on building strength and power. You’ll move through timed circuits of resistance-based exercises designed to torch calories, increase muscle endurance, and boost overall fitness in an efficient, full-body workout.
MixxedFit®  Dance Fitness
Cardio & Dance
MixxedFit® is a people-inspired dance fitness program. It is a mix of explosive dancing and body weight exercises to help you tone and sculpt your way through each song. You will hear multiple genres of music from hip hop, pop, rock, reggae, and everything in between, making the class fly by. During this 60 min class, you will experience a workout that is not only easy to follow but makes fitness FUN.
Pilates
Mind & Body
Pilates is a core class designed to train strength, flexibility, muscular endurance, coordination, balance, and good posture. The focus is on muscles of the core and those that attach to the spine, front and back - called the "Powerhouse" in Pilates.
Power Yoga
Mind & Body
Power yoga is a dynamic and more physically demanding form of yoga that focuses on building strength, flexibility, and stamina through a vigorous flow of poses. It allows for more variation in sequences and prioritizes a fitness-oriented approach.
Les Mills SHAPES™
A dynamic, low-impact workout that blends Pilates, barre, and power yoga for a full-body burn. SHAPES™ sculpts and strengthens your muscles, improves posture, and enhances flexibility, all in a fun, music-driven class designed to leave you feeling strong and centered.
Strength & Mobility
A total-body functional strength class that improves strength, balance, and mobility using practical movements for everyday life and athletic performance.
Stretch & Restore
Mind & Body
Stretch & Restore is a rejuvenating fitness class designed to improve flexibility, release tension, and promote relaxation. Through a blend of gentle stretches and restorative movements, this class helps reduce stress, enhance mobility, and support recovery. Perfect for all fitness levels, it’s an ideal way to unwind and recharge your body and mind.
Total Body Strength
Strength & Conditioning
Total Body Strength is a full-body workout that uses dumbbells, kettlebells, exercise balls, and other equipment. Using creative muscle-toning exercises, each class will target the upper body, lower body, and core to deliver a well-rounded workout.
WERQ® Dance Fitness
Cardio & Dance
WERQ® is a wildly addictive cardio dance workout based on pop and hip hop music and fresh dance fitness moves. The signature WERQ® warm-up previews the dance steps used in class and the signature WERQ® cool-down includes balance and yoga inspired poses
Yin Yoga
Mind & Body
Yin yoga is a slow-paced, meditative style of yoga that focuses on the body's connective tissues. Join us for a 1-hour calming yoga practice that will challenge you in new ways as you hold postures for extended periods of time to work deep into your muscles and fascia
Yoga Flow
Mind & Body
Yoga Flow is a vinyasa style yoga class that emphasizes the sequential movement between postures, coordinated with and guided by deliberate breath. There is a heightened focus on linking conscious breath with mindful fluid movement. Expect a fully balanced class of forward bending, twists, backbends with opportunity for inversions. All levels are welcome to join!
Zumba Dance Fitness
Cardio & Dance
Zumba is a fun, high-energy workout that combines Latin dance moves with upbeat music. You'll be having way too much fun to realize you're working out! Please note this class takes place in Waitzer Hall, on the Macon & Joan Brock Virginia Health Sciences at Old Dominion University campus
