Group Tours at ODU
Exploring college as a group? We welcome school and community organizations to visit Old Dominion University and experience what it's like to be part of Monarch Nation. Group tours are designed for 10 or more attendees and are offered by request on Tuesdays and Thursdays at 11:00 AM and 1:00 PM. To accommodate your group, we recommend registering in advance. For special circumstances or additional questions, feel free to reach out to us at visit@odu.edu .
