Will My Credits Transfer to ODU?
Your enrollment counselor will be able to answer all of your transfer questions. To take a look at where you may stand in the meantime, check out our course equivalency tool . Here, you'll find equivalencies for all courses that transfer to ODU from any accredited U.S. college or university.
If your courses aren't listed in our course equivalency tool, please contact us or reach out to your ODUGlobal enrollment counselor to see whether those credits will apply.

What if I Have Credits from a Virginia Community College?
Old Dominion University and the Virginia Community College System (VCCS) have a guaranteed admission agreement designed so that qualified VCCS students can make a seamless transfer to ODU.
Want to learn more about dual enrollment? Contact us with the form above or send an email to oduglobal@odu.edu to discuss your transfer opportunities.
You can also check out the VCCS Transfer Guide to learn more.
Not Sure Where to Start?
There's a lot to consider when starting an online bachelor's degree program. Our enrollment counselors can answer your questions and help you create a plan to take classes in a way that saves you time and money.
Whether you're transferring or starting a new bachelor's degree, we will help you every step of the way.
