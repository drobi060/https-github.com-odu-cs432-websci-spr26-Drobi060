Leo Online
Image
Leo Online is ODU's student and employee self-service portal, offering secure, role-based access to official academic and administrative records. Through its modern, mobile-friendly interface, you can manage personal records, view grades and transcripts, register for classes, access payroll and benefits, submit final grades, and more. Leo Online streamlines essential tasks and supports a transparent, efficient campus experience.
