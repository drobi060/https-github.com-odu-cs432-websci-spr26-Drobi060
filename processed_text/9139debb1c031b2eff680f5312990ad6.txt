Open House
Experience Old Dominion University
Discover what it means to be a Monarch at Old Dominion University’s Open House! Whether you're just starting your college search or making your final decision, our Open House events give you an inside look at life at ODU.
What to Expect:
Explore Campus: Take a guided tour of our beautiful waterfront campus and see where Monarchs live, learn, and thrive.
Meet the ODU Community: Connect with current students, faculty, and staff who are here to support your journey.
Learn About Academic Programs: With 175+ programs, you’ll find the perfect fit for your goals.
Get Admissions & Financial Aid Info: Learn about scholarships, housing, and everything you need to plan for your future.
