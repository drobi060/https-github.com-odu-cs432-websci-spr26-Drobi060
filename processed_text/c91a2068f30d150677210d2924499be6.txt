Employer Partners
Partner With Us
The Center for Career and Leadership Development Employer Partner Program offers an opportunity for employers to demonstrate their commitment and financial support to Old Dominion University. Partner with us to support career education while enhancing your recruitment efforts of Monarchs.
For inquiries about supporting the Center for Career and Leadership Development at any partner level, please contact Dr. Mary Smith, Associate Director for Employer Programs and Development at m19smith@odu.edu .
