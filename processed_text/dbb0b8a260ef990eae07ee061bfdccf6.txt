Fitness
We're here to help you become a Moving Monarch! Try attending a weekly fitness class at the Student Recreation Center (SRC) -- from Strength & Conditioning to Yoga, there's something for you! Or take advantage of our hands-on informative experiences on nutrition, wellness and fitness. Sign up for a personal trainer, or explore a new dimension of fitness with Small Group Training which merges group fitness with personal training. Start planning a semester of fitness today!
Getting Started
Whether you're a beginner or experienced athlete, our trained Fitness Specialists can help you reach your goals. Start here to kickstart your fitness journey, or fine-tune your current routine to optimize your results!
Fitness Orientation Body Fat Analysis
Interested in working out at the gym, but don't know where to start? Sign up for a free fitness orientation! One of our Fitness Specialists will show you around the SRWC fitness areas, introduce you to a few pieces of exercise equipment, and tell you about our Fitness and Wellness offerings. An optional Body Fat Analysis can also be provided to give you baseline data to start on your fitness goals! Sign Up Online
Late Policy: If you are more than 10 minutes late your session will count as a no-show.
No Show Policy: Sessions have a finite number of time slots available. To be fair to all participants, if you do not cancel and do not show up to your session, you are considered a "no-show" participant. If you no-show two times in one semester, you will lose your online program registration privileges for a month.
