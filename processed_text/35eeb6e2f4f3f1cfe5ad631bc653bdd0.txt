Cancellation Policy
The Center for Career & Leadership Development sets cancellation deadlines for each career fair/event which are typically 2 to 3 weeks before the event date.  These are identified on the Handshake registration page for each individual event.  All cancellations must be submitted in writing via email to employers@odu.edu .  No cancellations will be accepted over the phone or in-person.  Organizations cancelling before the cancellation deadline will receive a refund minus the $50 non-refundable processing fee.  If your organization encounters an extenuating circumstance, please contact the Employer Relations team for additional information.
Rain or Shine Policy
The Center for Career & Leadership Development hosts several career fairs throughout the year and all events will occur rain or shine.  In the event a career fair has to be cancelled or rescheduled due to extreme weather or a University mandated closure, any amount already paid by the organization will be applied to the rescheduled date or a career fair of the equivalent size and scope occurring in the next semester (or year if the event is only held once per academic year).  If you would like to request a refund, please submit your request in writing via email to employers@odu.edu .
No Show Policy
Organizations that do not check in by the start of the career fair on the day of the event will be considered a “no show.”   The Center for Career & Leadership Development reserves the right to restrict access to recruiting activities due to perpetual no shows.
