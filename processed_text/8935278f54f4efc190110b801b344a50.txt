Why choose ODU Science?
The College of Sciences at Old Dominion University is committed to providing undergrad and graduate degree programs of the highest quality. Our objective is to create and maintain a supportive environment where students can learn and discover.
Science majors have the opportunity to pursue research or internships in their field of study, giving them substantial exposure to the real world and important competitive advantages for their future careers. Our undergraduate programs are designed to:
Provide an exceptional academic experience
Prepare students for an increasingly competitive workplace
Encourage creativity and innovation
Promote career development opportunities
The College of Sciences at ODU values and favors both teaching and research. As a Carnegie "R1" University, we are proud to be among the top 5% of institutions nationwide for "very high research activity."
