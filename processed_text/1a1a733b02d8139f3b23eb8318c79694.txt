Alumni Opportunities
At CCLD, we’re here to support you—whether you’re a recent graduate taking your first steps into the professional world or a seasoned professional exploring new career opportunities. Our dedicated team provides personalized guidance tailored to your career and leadership goals, ensuring that you have the resources and support you need at every stage of your journey.
Our Services for Alumni Include:
Personalized Career Coaching:
Receive expert advice and personalized career support from one of our experienced career coaches. Schedule a one-on-one consultation through Handshake using the Career Center tab to get started. Virtual appointments are available via Handshake for your convenience.
Job Search Assistance:
Explore our comprehensive job database on HANDSHAKE , where you can search and apply for hundreds of entry-level, mid-career, and professional roles across industries.
Career Resources Hub:
Access a wide range of tools to enhance your job search, including resume guides, interview tips, networking strategies, and more. Whether you’re preparing for a career change or moving up in your field, our resources will help you stand out.
Career Fairs and Networking Events:
Attend our virtual and in-person career fairs to connect with employers and expand your network.
Take the next step in your career journey with confidence—schedule your career coaching session today and unlock the full potential of our career services.
