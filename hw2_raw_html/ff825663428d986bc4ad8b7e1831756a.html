Housing & Residence Life

Get the most out of your college experience by living with us.

There is a place for you in our on-campus residence halls or university apartments. Check our calendar to learn about important dates and events. ODU Residence Halls remain open during breaks with the exception of winter. University Village and Powhatan Apartments will remain open during all breaks. Apply Now ▶

Meet Our Counselor in-Residence

Dr. Bonita is a dedicated mental health professional available right where you live. She offers confidential support, guidance, and wellness strategies to help you navigate stress, academics, relationships, and everyday challenges. When booking your session, be sure to choose the "Villages Satellite Office" option.
