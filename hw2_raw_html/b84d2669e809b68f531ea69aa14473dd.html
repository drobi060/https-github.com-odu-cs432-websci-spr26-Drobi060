Holds

  • Cannot Register if Graduating (RG hold)
  • No Registration Time Ticket

Students who are graduating at the end of the current term will have a hold that prevents further registration.

If you still need to take courses to complete program requirements, e-mail graduation@odu.edu to notify the University Registrar of your new graduation term and to request that the hold be lifted.

If you wish to continue taking classes after you graduate, you must be readmitted as a degree-seeking or non-degree student. Once you have been admitted to a new degree program, or have been admitted to take courses as a non-degree student following graduation, the hold will be removed. If the hold remains after you have been admitted, e-mail register@odu.edu for assistance.

For complete information, see RG hold below.

This is not a hold, but does prevent registration during pre-registration only until such time as the student is eligible to register. Admitted students without time tickets may be eligible to register once open registration starts.

Only degree-seeking students registered for the current term are eligible to receive time tickets.

LEO Online

Students should always check LEO Online before registering, to be sure all holds have been cleared.

Holds are visible as soon as they are attached to students' accounts. Future holds are displayed in bold and become effective on the date specified.

Advising Staff

University policy requires all degree-seeking students to be advised, whether or not a major has been declared. Students must contact an advisor to consult on the degree completion plan in order for the hold to be lifted.

  • All new first-year students will meet with the advisor during Monarch Orientation if attending classes on the main campus.
  • ODU Online students must contact their assigned advisor.

     
  • New transfer students attending classes on the main campus should contact either the transfer advisor in the college or the designated faculty advisor.

     
  • Students attending classes at one of the ODU Higher Education Centers may contact the closest center for advising.

     
  • Graduate students must contact their academic departments to remove this hold.

     
  • These holds become effective either November 1 for registration for the next spring and summer semesters, or April 1 for registration for the following fall.

Hold Prohibits: Registration

Contact: View the advisor list at http://www.odu.edu/success/academic/advising/advisors, or e-mail advisor@odu.edu.

Office of Finance (Accounts Receivable)

A Financial Hold is placed on a student's account when a past-due balance exceeds $500. Please log into Leo Online using your MIDAS ID and password to review the account balance due. To remove the hold, students or authorized payers can make a payment online or in person. In most cases, the hold will automatically be removed within four hours of payment being received. 

Hold Prohibits: Registration, deferred payment arrangements, add/drop classes, and additional accounts receivable activity.

Contact: Office of Finance, Rollins Hall, 757-683-3030, tuition@odu.edu

A Student Account Hold is a hold placed on a student’s account when certain financial related matters have occurred. This could include matters related to returned checks and accounts referred to a collection agency. The hold restricts access to registration and will remain in place until the matter is resolved. 

Hold Prohibits: Registration, deferred payment arrangements, add/drop classes, and additional accounts receivable activity.

Contact: Student Accounts/Bursar Office, 757-683-3030, tuition@odu.edu

Athletics

The AT hold is the primary hold used by Athletics. Student-athletes are associated with their sport code in the administrative system beginning with orientation. This hold is placed by the athletic advising staff and used to prevent student-athletes or staff on campus from changing registration schedules for the student-athletes, ensuring full-time enrollment of at least 12 hours and fulfilling other University and NCAA requirements. The Graduate School

All degree-seeking and post-baccalaureate (teacher licensure) graduate students admitted or readmitted since Fall 2010 are required to complete training in the fundamentals of Responsible Conduct of Research (RCR). This training must be completed prior to the completion of 12 semester hours. Students who do not complete the RCR training will have a hold preventing registration and graduation until the training is completed. Students can check DegreeWorks for the entry showing that the training has been completed.

Hold Prohibits: Registration, Graduation

Questions relating to the RCR training should be addressed to Wayne Hynes, Interim Director of Research Compliance, at whynes@odu.edu or 757-683-3613.

If the student's record has not been updated or the hold has not been removed after three business days from the date of completion of the training, contact: Office of the University Registrar, register@odu.edu.

Hold Prohibits: Registration

Assessment Office

Freshmen students are required to complete a transition to college inventory (TCI) questionnaire in advance of attending the University. The TCI helps staff at the University to understand characteristics of students in the class in order to facilitate their transition to the University. The TCI is normally completed during Preview. Students who do not attend Preview will have the TCI hold placed and will not be able to register.
Hold Prohibits: Registration
Contact: Office of Assessment, 757-683-3322, assess@odu.edu

Freshmen students are required to complete the Freshman Assessment. This is a survey that must be submitted in advance of registration for the student's second semester at the University.
Hold Prohibits: Registration
Contact: Office of Assessment, 757-683-3322, assess@odu.edu

Graduate students are required to complete the Graduate Student Satisfaction Survey (GSSS) during their last semester at the University. The degree will not be posted and the student will not be sent the diploma until the GSSS has been completed and submitted to the Assessment Office.
Hold Prohibits: Graduation
Contact: Office of Assessment, 757-683-3322, assess@odu.edu

Transfer students are required to complete the Transfer Student Satisfaction Survey during the first semester of attendance. Students will not be able to register for a second semester if the transfer student assessment has not been completed. Students will receive a personal invitation at their ODU email address shortly after the beginning of the Fall or Spring semester.
Hold Prohibits: Registration
Contact: Office of Assessment, 757-683-3322, assess@odu.edu

Entering transfer students will be sent an invitation to take the Transfer Biographical Questionnaire (BioQ) after they have completed the Transfer Student Satisfaction Survey. Students will not be able to register for a second semester if the transfer student assessment has not been completed. Students will receive a personal invitation at their ODU email address within 24 hours after submitting the TSS.
Hold Prohibits: Registration
Contact: Office of Assessment, 757-683-3322, assess@odu.edu

The Senior Assessment (Senior Student Satisfaction Survey) is a University requirement for graduation. It must be completed during the semester in which the student plans to graduate and is available to students who have applied for graduation and are "pending" for graduation in the records of the Registrar's Office. A personal invitation will be sent to the student's ODU email address. Students who have not completed the Senior Assessment can participate in commencement ceremonies, but the degree will not be posted to the record and the diploma will be withheld until the survey has been completed and submitted to the Office of Assessment.
Hold Prohibits: Graduation
Contact: Office of Assessment, 757-683-3322, assess@odu.edu

Writing Placement and Support

This hold was placed on the records of students who transferred in credit for English 110C and were required to take the Writing Sample Placement Test (WSPT) prior to or during the first semester at the University. If they passed, they were to have met the initial writing requirement. If they did not pass, they were required to meet with a Writing Center counselor to review their test. If students did not take or had not reviewed the test at the end of their first semester, a hold was placed on registration for the next semester. This policy has been discontinued. Any student with a WC hold may have it removed by a university official authorized to release holds or may contact writingplacement@odu.edu to request its removal.

Hold Prohibits: Registration

Contact: Writing Placement and Support, email: writingplacement@odu.edu, https://www.odu.edu/english/general-education/writing-placement
