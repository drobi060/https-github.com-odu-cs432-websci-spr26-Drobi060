Use this form if you would like to change the campus you are currently assigned to. The most common reasons to change campuses are: lifestyle/fit, military deployment, permanent relocation, desired program change, temporary relocation for summer, and correcting a campus code error.

If you are an on-campus student and you wish to take the rest of your program on-line – request to change your campus code to Online Campus Student in VA, Online Campus Student outside of VA, or Online Campus Student outside of US.

If you are an online student and you wish to take the rest of your program on-campus – request to change your campus code to Norfolk Main Campus Student

If you are an on-campus student and you will relocate outside of Virginia or the U.S. during the summer but plan to return to on-campus in the fall – use the Summer Campus Change Request form to request a temporary campus change for the summer only.

If you intend to study from a location out-of-state or outside the United States – you should request to change your campus code to "online student outside Virginia" or "online student outside USA," as you will be eligible for the tuition rate applicable to students enrolled in technology delivered courses from these locations.

An active MIDAS ID is needed to access the Campus Change Request Form. Students who previously attended ODU should visit the Returning Student page for guidance on how to reactivate their student account prior to requesting a campus change.
