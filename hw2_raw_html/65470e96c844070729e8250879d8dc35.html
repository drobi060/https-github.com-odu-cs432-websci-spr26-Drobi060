AP, IB, Cambridge, & CAPE Credit

Test scores can be sent directly from the testing agency. Send your AP, IB, and Cambridge Advanced A/AS exam scores to ODU through the College Board using the College Examination Entrance Board (CEEB) code: 005126.

For tests not listed, please contact the Transfer Center for assistance with a departmental review at transfereval@odu.edu.
