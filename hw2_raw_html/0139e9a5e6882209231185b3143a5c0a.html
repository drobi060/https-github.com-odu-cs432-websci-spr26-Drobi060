Transfer Admissions

Finish with ODUGlobal

We want to help you finish your bachelor’s degree online. On your own time and fast. Our enrollment team is ready to help you through the admissions process!

As an ODUGlobal student, you'll have a dedicated Student Success Advisor that stays with you all the way to graduation. They’ll help you build a plan, stay on track, and connect with the resources you need to thrive.

If you are interested in pursuing an undergraduate degree, we can provide a free unofficial evaluation of your domestic transcript(s).





We make it easy to see how your credits can transfer!

  • Submit your transcripts for a free unofficial evaluation before you apply. Start from any inquiry form on our website!
  • We'll consider college-level courses with a grade of C or better earned from regionally accredited institutions.
  • Military service member or veteran? We'll also check your military experience and Joint Services Transcript for transfer credits. Plus, we award academic credit for ranks E6-E9.

You don't need an associate degree to transfer. Whether you attended a few classes or several colleges, we'll help you take advantage of the work you've already completed.

We're Making it Easier to Transfer

You could earn Guaranteed Admission to ODU! 

We've approved transfer-oriented associate degrees from college systems across the U.S. to make it easier to finish your bachelor's degree. We'll even help you prepare for transfer!

Learn More About Guaranteed Admission

If you decide not to use Guaranteed Admission, earning a transfer-oriented associate degree from an approved school could still satisfy ODU's lower level general education requirements—up to 60 credits! The total time to complete your degree will depend on how your credits apply toward degree program requirements.

Your experience and training can count for academic credit at ODU, whether you're active duty or a veteran. ODUGlobal's unique military pathways incorporate credit that may be earned outside the classroom through exams, experience, and/or training. 

Learn More about Military Pathways to Bachelor's Degrees

Get Undergraduate Credit for Ranks E6-E9

ODU has agreements with the Virginia Community College System and select other colleges across the country that make it easier to transfer to ODU. And you may be able to start taking ODU classes while you finish your associate degree.

Have Questions? We have answers.

Your enrollment counselor will be able to answer all of your transfer questions. To take a look at where you may stand in the meantime, check out our course equivalency tool. Here, you'll find equivalencies for all courses that transfer to ODU from any accredited U.S. college or university.

If your courses aren't listed in our course equivalency tool, please contact us or reach out to your ODUGlobal enrollment counselor to see whether those credits will apply.

You've talked to an enrollment counselor. You have a plan for success. It's time to apply! Remember, your enrollment counselor can help you through every step of the application process.

Old Dominion University and the Virginia Community College System (VCCS) have a guaranteed admission agreement designed so that qualified VCCS students can make a seamless transfer to ODU.

Want to learn more about dual enrollment? Contact us with the form above or send an email to oduglobal@odu.edu to discuss your transfer opportunities.

You can also check out the VCCS Transfer Guide to learn more.

There's a lot to consider when starting an online bachelor's degree program. Our enrollment counselors can answer your questions and help you create a plan to take classes in a way that saves you time and money.

Whether you're transferring or starting a new bachelor's degree, we will help you every step of the way.
