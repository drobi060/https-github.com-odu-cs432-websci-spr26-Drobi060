ODU Launches New Doctor of Applied Science in Kinesiology

The Ellmer College of Health Sciences at ODU is proud to introduce its newest doctoral offering: the fully online, cohort-based Doctor of Applied Science (DAS) in Kinesiology. Built for working professionals with a kinesiology-related master’s or clinical doctorate, this innovative program is designed to elevate careers through practical expertise in teaching, leadership, and communication. ODU MLS Summer Immersion Program

Explore the exciting world of Medical Laboratory Science through this hands-on, immersive summer experience at Old Dominion University. Designed for students ages 16 and older, the MLS Summer Immersion Program introduces participants to careers in clinical laboratories, with a special focus on blood bank and core laboratory disciplines.

Need Advising?

The Health Sciences Advising Center assist students who intend to apply to academic programs in the Ellmer College of Health Sciences and Ellmer School of Nursing. Each semester students are required to meet with an advisor to ensure that you are on track toward applying to your intended program.
