Memberships

From January 1 through February 28, ODU employees, alumni, and approved affiliates are invited to sign up for a one week free trial membership to the ODU Student Recreation and Well-Being Center!

Ready to start your membership?

All memberships must be started in-person at the front desk of the Student Recreation & Well-Being Center or Waitzer Hall Wellness Center. Please visit us with a current, government-issued picture ID to complete your application, make payment, and get started on your fitness journey!

Membership Add-Ons

Long-term locker rentals are available for members looking for a more convenient gym experience. Please visit the Student Recreation & Well-Being Center front desk to get started with your first locker rental.

  • Recreation & Wellness provides a lock for you to use for the duration of the locker rental.
  • Individuals who purchase a locker rental must follow all related policies.
  • Includes complementary towel service!
Per semester $25
Annually $60
LOCKER RENEWAL

At the end of your rental period, you will be sent a reminder to renew your locker rental online or remove your items by the given date. Any items left in a locker after the rental period has expired will be placed at the Equipment Desk Lost & Found for 30 days. After 30 days, any unclaimed items will be discarded. Renew Locker►

Towel service options are available for purchase online in the RecWell Portal or at the SRWC Front Desk. Members with a long-term locker rental receive complementary towel service.

  • Simply visit the Equipment Desk to pick up your towel.
  • Please remember to return your towel before the end of the day to avoid equipment fees.
Day towel $1
Semester towel service $15
Annual towel service $35
Long-term locker renters free
