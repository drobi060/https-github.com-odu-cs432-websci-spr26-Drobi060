Click here to review pre-approved LeADERS Leadership courses.

Pre-Approved Co-curricular Options:

  • Army ROTC
  • CIVIC Scholars
  • Freshman Summer Institute
  • Leadership Certificate from the LSI Leadership Lecture Series
  • Navy ROTC
  • Monarchs LEAD: ODU Comprehensive Leadership Program

Candidates may notify the program of completed Leadership co-curricular experiences by visiting the LeADERS Canvas page and submitting a Degree Works Update Request form.

Pre-approved elective options:

  • Learning Communities
  • Annual Campus Theme
  • Capstone Courses
  • Civic Engagement Experiences

In addition to the pre-approved elective options above, students may self-identify a high-impact course or co-curricular experience that was particularly meaningful to them and earn progress towards their final LeADERS distinction level.

Candidates may notify the program of completed elective experiences by visiting the LeADERS Canvas page and submitting a Degree Works Update Request form.
