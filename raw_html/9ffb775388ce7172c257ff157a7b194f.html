Office of Intercultural Relations

Vision

To foster campus interculturalization through initiatives that integrate intercultural learning experiences, embrace local and global perspectives, and strengthen students' sense of belonging, well-being, engagement, and success.

Campus interculturalization refers to the process of promoting meaningful interaction and exchange within the campus community. It promotes understanding by considering both local and global perspectives and contexts, facilitating a collaborative environment that emphasizes shared learning and mutual respect.

Mission

To empower students to navigate an interconnected world by fostering belonging, cultural well-being, and community through collaboration, cultural engagement, and experiential learning opportunities. Guided by its four pillars—learning, engagement, well-being, and belonging—OIR strengthens student experiences and supports the university’s core values of accessibility, collaboration, excellence, global engagement, inclusion, innovation, and respect.

 

Core Values

OIR is dedicated to creating accessible opportunities for members of the ODU community to engage with its programs, services, and resources.

OIR fosters meaningful partnerships across campus and beyond, working closely with students, faculty, staff, and community organizations to create impactful initiatives.

OIR strives for excellence in its programs and initiatives through innovation and high impact within the ODU community.

OIR promotes intercultural awareness and global engagement by connecting the ODU community to opportunities that develop knowledge and skills to understand cultures and perspectives.

OIR is committed to fostering an environment where individuals feel welcomed, valued, and celebrated for their contributions.

OIR embraces creativity and forward-thinking approaches in its programs and initiatives to address the evolving needs of a diverse and dynamic campus community.

OIR cultivates a culture of care and mutual respect within the ODU community.
