Joint School of Public Health

The Joint School of Public Health is a collaborative partnership between Old Dominion University and Norfolk State University to offer experiential learning and research opportunities across multiple campuses. The first of its kind in Virginia, students learn through hands-on experience, classroom learning, and community engagement to prepare for the public health jobs of the future.

Transforming Public Health in Virginia

The Joint School of Public Health focuses on community engagement, addressing health disparities, and advancing research to improve public health outcomes in eastern and coastal Virginia.
