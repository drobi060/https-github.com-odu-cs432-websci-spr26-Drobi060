Digital Ethics and ePortfolio Use

As with any form of digital composition, ePortfolio creation and use poses some risks. These risks can potentially be addressed or alleviated in various ways. The Association for Authentic, Experiential, and Evidence-Based Learning (AAEEBL) offers guidance for identifying and navigating these ethical issues via AAEEBL's Digital Ethics Principles.
